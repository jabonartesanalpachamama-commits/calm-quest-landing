# Design System

## Theme
Light theme is default. A calm, warm, soft-sand tone `#FAF8F5` inspired by morning tea, linen textures, and natural light to transmit warmth, serenity, and professional safety.

## Color Strategy
*   **Restrained & Nature-Inspired**: 4 original + 3 new dynamic palettes (Menta, Lavanda, Tierra, Océano, Loto, Prana, Savia) with natural earth-grounded HSL variables.
*   **No Pure Black or White**: Neutrals are tinted with green/purple/brown tones depending on the selected palette (e.g. background `#F7F4EF`, card `#FFFFFF`).

## Typography
*   **Heading Font**: `Cormorant Garamond` or `Lora` (elegant Serif, serif dynamic pairing) for premium editorial feel.
*   **Body Font**: `Lora` or clean Sans-serif (like Inter or Outfit) for optimal legibility.
*   **Scale**: Heading sizes ≥1.25x ratio (e.g., Title: 3rem, Subtitle: 1.5rem, Body: 1rem). Base body text is 16px to prevent mobile iOS auto-zoom.

## Components
*   **Forms**: Clean inputs with thin `#EBE7DF` borders, smooth transition on focus to primary brand color.
*   **Buttons**: Round-full pill shape with smooth transition triggers (`transition-all duration-300`). Zero emojis on main control actions.
*   **Cards**: Organic, round borders (`rounded-3xl` / `rounded-2xl`) with very soft, warm shadows (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md` tinted with green/brown) to represent smooth pebbles or stones.

## Spacing & Spacing Scale
*   **8dp Rhythm**: Content spacing and padding incremented in 4/8dp scale (e.g., padding `p-4`, `p-6`, `p-8`, margins `space-y-6`, `space-y-8`).
*   **Horizontal Gutters**: Comfortable edge margins `px-6` on mobile, scaling to `max-w-6xl` centered on larger displays.
